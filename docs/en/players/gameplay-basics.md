# Gameplay Basics

This page explains what is different about BeamNG.drive when you play on a BeamMP server. The settings behind each part are in [Multiplayer Settings](/en/players/multiplayer-settings).

## The player list

The in-game player list shows each player's name and ping. You can also show each player's ID, which is useful for server staff. Clicking a player's name spectates them, or loads their queued changes: you choose which mouse button does which in the settings.

## Nametags

Each player has a nametag above their vehicle, and it can carry a role tag. You can hide nametags, show the distance to each player, and fade them in or out with distance.

## Chat

Chat is either in a window that you can drag out of the game (the new chat menu) or in the UI app.

## Other players' vehicles

- Players' vehicles appear in your game, and their part changes are synced to you. You can send your own part changes to others with the part sync button in the part picker, or have them sent automatically.
- Press `Tab` to switch between vehicles. You can skip other players' vehicles.
- A vehicle that has not spawned for you yet shows as a coloured placeholder orb, called a blob.
- To protect your build, turn on config cloning protection so other players cannot save your vehicle's config.

## The event queue

When another player spawns or edits a vehicle, your game can queue the change instead of loading it at once, so a load does not interrupt your driving. Queued changes load when you click the **Events** button at the top of the screen, when you click the player's name, or automatically when you have been driving slowly for long enough. See [the event queue settings](/en/players/multiplayer-settings#event-queue).

## Servers with mods

When you connect to a server that has mods, BeamMP shows a mod security warning first. Read [Mod Safety](/en/players/mod-safety) before you accept it.
