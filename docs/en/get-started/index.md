# Get Started

BeamMP adds multiplayer to BeamNG.drive. This section takes you from nothing installed to driving on a server with other players.

## What you need

- A legitimate, up-to-date copy of BeamNG.drive.
- Windows or Linux. macOS support is being worked on. Linux and macOS are secondary platforms, so expect some bugs.

::: warning
BeamMP does not work with pirated or outdated copies of BeamNG.drive, and the support team cannot help with problems on them.
:::

## The three steps

1. [Install BeamMP](/en/get-started/install-beammp): download the installer (Windows) or build the Launcher (Linux).
2. [Join your first server](/en/get-started/join-first-server): start the Launcher, open multiplayer and connect.
3. [Check the first-time settings](/en/get-started/multiplayer-settings-quickstart): the few multiplayer settings worth knowing about before your first session.

## If something goes wrong

- The [Troubleshooting](/en/troubleshooting/) pages cover Launcher updates, connection problems and error codes.
- The [Player FAQ](/en/players/faq) answers the most common questions.
- You can ask on the [forum](https://forum.beammp.com) or on the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/beammp).
