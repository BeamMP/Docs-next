# Your BeamMP Account

This page is for players. It explains how to create a BeamMP account, sign in, keep it secure and manage it at [BeamMP Accounts](https://accounts.beammp.com).

The same account signs you in to the Launcher, the forum and Keymaster, where server owners manage their AuthKeys.

## Create an account

1. Open [BeamMP Accounts](https://accounts.beammp.com) and click **Register**.
2. Enter a username, your email address and a password, then confirm the password.
3. Complete the verification challenge and click **Create Account**. By registering you agree to the [terms of service](https://beammp.com/terms) and the [privacy policy](https://beammp.com/privacy).
4. Open the email from BeamMP and click the verification link. You cannot sign in before you verify your email address.

Your username has 4 to 20 characters: letters, numbers and underscores. Some usernames and some email providers are not allowed.

Your password has at least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a special character from `!@#$%^&*`.

::: info
BeamMP can close new registrations for a short time, for example during maintenance. Existing accounts can still sign in.
:::

## Sign in

1. Open [BeamMP Accounts](https://accounts.beammp.com).
2. Enter your username or your email address, and your password.
3. Complete the verification challenge and click **Sign In**.
4. If you turned on two-factor authentication, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app, or one of your recovery codes.

In the Launcher you sign in with the same username or email address and password.

### I forgot my password

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your email address and click **Send Reset Link**.
3. Open the email and follow the link to choose a new password.

### My email is not verified

When you sign in with an unverified email address, BeamMP sends you a new verification link. After you sign in, the home page shows **Email not verified** with a **Resend verification email** button.

### My account needs to be recovered

Some older accounts must be recovered before they can sign in. The sign-in page then tells you so.

1. On the sign-in page, click **Start Recovery**.
2. Enter your account email address and click **Recover Account**.
3. Follow the instructions in the email BeamMP sends you.

If you get no email, check your spam folder and try again later. If you still cannot sign in, create an **Account Support** ticket on [Discord](https://discord.gg/beammp).

## Your profile

Open the user menu in the top right and click **Your Profile**. The **General** tab has these options:

- **Change**, next to your picture, uploads an avatar. Use a JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF image of at most 5 MB. A square image of at least 256 by 256 pixels works best.
- **Bio** is a short text of up to 280 characters.
- **Secondary email** adds one optional backup address. BeamMP asks you to verify it.
- **Change email** sends a verification link to the new address. Your email address changes only after you verify it.

### Change your username

Username changes need approval from a moderator. Changes to capital and lowercase letters only, such as `beammp` to `BeamMP`, apply at once.

1. Open **Your Profile** and go to **Username change request**.
2. Enter the username you want. You can add a reason for the moderator.
3. Click **Submit Request**.

You can have one request waiting at a time. The result is in your **Notifications**, and **Latest request** shows the status. **Former usernames** lists your earlier names. The rules for usernames are the same as when you create an account.

## Security

Open **Your Profile** and go to the **Security** tab.

### Change your password

Enter your current password and the new password twice, then click **Update Security**. If you use two-factor authentication, enter a code too.

### Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication asks for a code from an authenticator app when you sign in to BeamMP Accounts.

1. In the **Security** tab, click **Enable 2FA**.
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app, or type the key into the app.
3. Enter the code from the app and click **Confirm**.
4. Save the 10 recovery codes BeamMP shows you, then click **I've saved these**. BeamMP does not show them again.

Each recovery code works once, if you lose your authenticator app. **Regenerate recovery codes** makes a new set. **Disable 2FA** turns it off. Both ask for your password and a current code.

### Active sessions

The **Security** tab lists the devices that are signed in to your account. Click **Revoke** to sign one out, or **Revoke All** to sign out every device except this one.

## Linked accounts {#linked-accounts}

Open **Your Profile** and go to **Linked Accounts**. Click **Link** next to a service and finish the sign-in in the window that opens. Click **Unlink** to remove the link at any time.

- **Discord** brings your old server keys to your account. It also lets BeamMP give you the extra server keys of a Discord Server Boost.
- **Patreon** connects your Patreon membership, so BeamMP gives you your supporter perks. A Patreon account can be linked to one BeamMP account.

See the [Player FAQ](/en/players/faq) for early access and Patreon perks.

## Your settings

Open the user menu in the top right and click **Settings**.

- **Language** and **Timezone** change how BeamMP Accounts shows text and times. **Auto Detect** uses your browser timezone.
- **Primary Group** chooses which of your groups, such as Early Access, sets the role BeamMP shows for you in game. **Leave** removes you from a group.
- **Download Data Export** gives you the personal data of your account as a JSON file.
- **Request Account Deletion** sends a confirmation link to your primary email address. Your account is deleted only after you open that link.

::: danger
Deleting your account is permanent. It removes your profile, your Patreon and Discord links and your server keys, and it cannot be undone.
:::

## Home and notifications

The **Home** page shows your account ID, your timezone and the servers you joined last. If you are an Early Access supporter, it says so.

The **Notifications** page lists messages from BeamMP, such as the result of a username change request or a suspension appeal. It also has your notification settings: email for security alerts, product updates and occasional marketing, and in-app notification pop-ups.

If your account is suspended, see [Suspensions and Appeals](/en/players/suspensions-and-appeals).
