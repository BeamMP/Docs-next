# Server Owners

Everything you need to host and look after a BeamMP server.

## Start here

Choose how you will host:

- **At home, on your own computer.** It is free. Follow [Host a Server](/en/server-owners/host-a-server). Players outside your home can only join if you [forward a port](/en/server-owners/port-forwarding).
- **On a VPS or with a hosting company.** It is easier and more secure, and needs no router changes. Follow [Server Setup on VPS](/en/server-owners/setup-vps).

## Look after your server

- [Server Configuration](/en/server-owners/configuration): the `ServerConfig.toml` settings, server names and tags, and the stock map paths.
- [Server Maintenance](/en/server-owners/maintenance): the log file and updating the server.
- [Server Manual](/en/server-owners/manual): environment variables, the Resources folder, command-line arguments and limits.

## When something goes wrong

- [Check for CGNAT](/en/server-owners/cgnat): why nobody can join even though ports are forwarded.
- [Server Error Codes](/en/server-owners/error-codes): what the codes in the Launcher window mean.
- [Server FAQ](/en/server-owners/faq): common questions and known issues.
