# Suspensions and Appeals

This page is for players whose BeamMP account is suspended. It explains what you can still do and how to appeal.

## What a suspension does

A moderator can suspend your account, with a reason and an end date. A suspension without an end date is permanent. When the end date passes, the suspension ends by itself.

While your account is suspended:

- You cannot sign in to BeamMP in game. The sign-in fails with a message that your account is suspended, until when or permanently.
- You cannot sign in to the forum. The message includes the moderator's reason.
- You can still sign in to [BeamMP Accounts](https://accounts.beammp.com), so that you can see the reason and appeal.

::: warning
Do not create a new BeamMP account to get around a suspension. The [community rules](/en/community/rules) forbid it.
:::

## See why you are suspended

Sign in to [BeamMP Accounts](https://accounts.beammp.com). The **Home** page shows an **Account Suspended** box with:

- The reason, if the moderator wrote one.
- The end date, as **This suspension is in effect until** a date and time, or **This suspension has no end date**. Times use the timezone in your account settings.

## Appeal a suspension

1. On the **Home** page, click **Appeal** in the **Account Suspended** box.
2. In **Appeal your suspension**, explain why the suspension should be lifted. You can write up to 2000 characters.
3. Click **Submit Appeal**. BeamMP sends a confirmation to your primary email address.

A member of staff reviews your appeal. You can have one appeal waiting at a time. While it waits, the box says that your appeal is under review.

When a moderator decides, you get a notification in BeamMP Accounts and an email:

- **Approved:** the suspension is lifted at once and you can sign in again.
- **Declined:** the suspension stays, and the message gives the moderator's reason.

After a declined appeal you must wait 12 hours before you appeal again. The box shows the time when you can submit the next one.

## Other bans

Other bans are appealed with the form on the [community rules](/en/community/rules) page.
